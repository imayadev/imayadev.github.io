🔬 Write for LabNotes: Science Communication Competition

LabNotes is inviting students aged 11–18 to submit original science articles for publication on our student-led science platform. This is your chance to share your passion for science and get published! 📚

✨ Key Highlights

  • Ages 11-14 (Junior) and 15-18 (Senior) categories
  • All accepted articles get published on our platform
  • Outstanding pieces are featured and highlighted
  • Final rankings published after the deadline
  • Every submission receives a digital certificate

🎯 Topic: 'Explain Real Science'

Choose a real scientific concept, process or discovery and explain it clearly for a non-specialist audience.

Topic Ideas:

Protein folding • CRISPR • Future fuels • Material science • Economic modelling • Energy systems • Applied physics • Quantum computing • Climate science • Neuroscience

Format: 700–1,200 words • Include photos or diagrams if helpful
